<Warning>Sync is not yet available.</Warning>

The planned design uses FastCDC chunks, BLAKE3 Merkle-DAG state, and deterministic conflict branching into separate `.conflict` files. Complete multi-host folder synchronization is not released.

CLI entries and internal components are development interfaces. Do not rely on them for production workflows.

Use [File Transfer](/guide/file-transfer) to send files and folders today.
